# Design and SEO review: cake-pan-converter

Reviewer: Miranda (independent). Build: `npm run build` OK (6 pages). Preview on port 4406, viewed with playwright-cli at 1280 and 390, light and dark. Server stopped.

## Scores (Lighthouse 13, headless Chrome, local preview)
| | Performance | Accessibility | Best practices | SEO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Desktop | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 |

LCP 1.1 s mobile (0.3 s desktop), CLS 0.02, TBT 0 ms. Page weight: 232 KB for the whole `dist`, home HTML 30 KB, one 22 KB CSS and one 15 KB JS file. One Lighthouse audit fails: label-content-name-mismatch (Swap button, see row 9).

## Verdict
Clearly better than the competitors in the research on the tool itself: volume not area only, pan count, to-scale drawing, pasted-list scaling with nice fractions, bake guide, share link, print, dark mode, no blank result. Look is clean and calm, brand colour is used well, dark mode is good, OG image is good. The known checks pass (8" to 9" round = 1.27x, 9x13 = 117 sq in, 9x13 to 12 cup bundt 0.74x, all 12 article-table factors re-computed and correct).

Gaps: three real bugs (unit toggle drift, ingredient parentheses, result below the fold on phones) and the SEO plan from the research (60 to 100 long-tail pages) is not built, so this is one page against many.

Must-fix count: 3 (rows 1 to 3). Rows 4 to 6 are strongly recommended before launch.

## Findings (max 25)
| # | Severity | Area | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Must-fix | Tool: units | Toggling Inches/Centimeters corrupts the pan. The code reads the rounded text in the fields back into state. 8" becomes 7.99", depth 2 becomes 2.01", 9 becomes 9.02", and every toggle repeats it. The shared URL then holds `7.992`. Same drift when a link with `u=cm` loads. | Do not call `readPan` after a unit change or at load; only read a field when the user edits it. Keep state in inches and write to fields only. Add a test: toggle 5 times, state unchanged. |
| 2 | Must-fix | Tool: ingredients | Text in parentheses is wrong or not scaled. `1 cup (2 sticks) butter` x1.27 gives `1 1/4 cups (2 sticks) butter`. `1 (14 oz) can milk` x0.86 gives `7/8 (12 oz) can milk`; x2 gives `2 (28 oz) can`. Sticks and "(14 oz) can" are very common in US recipes. | Scale `stick(s)` inside parentheses too. Do not rescale a size in parentheses that follows a bare count and precedes "can/package/jar/box". If unsure, leave the text and add the note "Check the amount in brackets." |
| 3 | Must-fix | Phone layout | The answer is 1,120 px down on a 390x844 phone. The user scrolls past both pan forms (two screens) to see the factor. Bad for one-thumb use. | Add a sticky compact bar at the bottom or under the header showing `1.27x` and "8" round to 9" round" once the forms are in view, with a "Jump to result" link. Or put the factor above the second form. Also tighten the pan form on phone (put Number of pans next to Depth). |
| 4 | High | SEO: long tail | The research planned 60 to 100 pre-rendered pages ("9x13 to 8 inch round", "two 8 inch rounds to 9x13"). Only one page exists (sitemap lists 5 URLs, 4 of them legal pages). Competitors win these queries by page. | Generate 40+ pages from `calc.js` with Astro `getStaticPaths`: own title, H1, answer in the first sentence, a pre-filled tool, a mini table, links to 5 related pages. Add them to the sitemap and link them from a "Common conversions" hub and the home table. |
| 5 | High | Drawing | The drawing shows one pan even when Number of pans is 2 or 3 (checked: 9x13 vs "2 x 8" round" shows one circle). Labels are 13 px in a 440 px box, so about 9.6 px on a phone; the caption is about 8.8 px. | Draw N pans (up to 4, then "x 6"). Raise label size to 16 and caption to 14 in viewBox units, or show names in HTML under the SVG. Add a short `<desc>`/text alternative with the sizes. |
| 6 | High | Tool: share link | Pasting a share link into a tab that already has the tool only changes the hash, so nothing updates (checked: stays 1.27x round). Back/forward has the same gap. | Listen to `hashchange`, parse the state, call `renderPan` and `update`. |
| 7 | Medium | Tool: ingredients | Grammar: `1 large egg` x1.27 gives `1 1/2 large egg`; x2 gives `2 large egg`. `1 egg yolk` gets the "beat 1 extra egg" note, which does not fit a yolk. | Pluralize "egg", "egg yolk" and "egg white" when the amount is above 1. Skip the half-egg note for yolks and whites (suggest rounding to a whole count). |
| 8 | Medium | Tool: empty state | "Scale your ingredients" shows a grey placeholder that looks like real text (placeholder is ink at 50%, about 3.2:1). The scaled list has no heading, so it reads like more input. No example button. | Add a "Try an example" link that fills the box. Label the output "Scaled for your pan" with a lighter card. Use a placeholder colour that is clearly lighter, but keep 4.5:1 for the label. Same for the 30/350 placeholders. |
| 9 | Medium | Accessibility | Swap button: visible text "Swap pans", `aria-label` "Swap the two pans". This is the one Lighthouse failure (WCAG 2.5.3 label in name). | Remove the `aria-label` (the text already works); hide the arrow glyph with `aria-hidden`. |
| 10 | Medium | Accessibility | The whole `#result` block is `aria-live="polite"` and the SVG is rebuilt on every keystroke, so a screen reader may read the number, table and drawing repeatedly. | Put `aria-live` only on a small visually hidden summary ("Multiply by 1.27. Your pan holds 27% more batter.") and update it after a short delay. |
| 11 | Medium | Validation | Error text always says "Enter a size for ..." even when the problem is Number of pans = 0 or depth = 0. While invalid, the old factor (stale `1.27x`) stays on screen at 35% opacity. No upper limit: 9999" gives `1562187.52x` and "156218652% more". | Name the real field ("Enter at least 1 pan", "Enter a depth"). Show a dash instead of the stale number. Warn above about 24" or 10x change ("Check the units, did you mean cm?"). |
| 12 | Medium | Tool: spec gaps | The research promised "warn when a pan would be more than two thirds full" and a muffin option. The tool only prints a fixed "fill no more than two thirds" line. | Add a fill check: if the batter volume (recipe pan volume x scale, from the user's batter guess) is more than 2/3 of the new pan, warn; or at least a warning when the scale is over 1.5x and the depth is under 2.5". Muffin/cupcake can wait. |
| 13 | Medium | Tap targets | Header links are 36 px high, footer links 20 px, FAQ summaries 24 px, unit buttons 40 px. Under the 44 px guide on a phone. | Give header links `py-3`, footer links `py-2.5` with a gap, FAQ summaries `py-1` (so about 44 px), unit buttons `h-11`. |
| 14 | Medium | SEO: copy | The meta description and the hero paragraph are the same sentence, and it starts with "Free cake pan converter." right under an H1 that says the same words. Reads stuffy. | Hero: "Pick the pan in your recipe and the pan you have. Get the scale factor, scaled ingredients and a bake time guide." Meta: add the hook, for example "8 inch to 9 inch round is 1.27x. Convert any pan: round, square, 9x13, loaf, bundt." (under 155 chars). |
| 15 | Medium | SEO: SERP | Title is 49 characters and fine, but has no number or conversion hook. No `lastmod` in the sitemap. | Keep the title; test "Cake Pan Converter: 9x13, Round, Square and Bundt" later in Search Console. Add `lastmod` via the sitemap config. |
| 16 | Low | SEO: schema | JSON-LD parses (WebApplication, FAQPage, 8 questions). Good. Missing `image`, `screenshot`, `featureList`, and no `WebSite` or `BreadcrumbList` for sub-pages. FAQ answers match the page text. | Add `featureList` and `image`. Do NOT add ratings. Add `BreadcrumbList` when the long-tail pages exist. |
| 17 | Low | SEO: meta | `<meta name="keywords">` is ignored by Google. | Remove it. |
| 18 | Low | SEO: article | Article is 749 words plus 8 FAQs (about 1,300 words in the page). Good depth for the main query. The text does not link to any other page, and the main H2 "How a cake pan converter works" sits under two tool H2s ("Bake time and temperature", "Scale your ingredients"), so the outline starts with tool labels. | Make the two tool headings `h3` (or styled `div`). Link "cake tin converter" and the conversion table rows to the long-tail pages when they exist. Add a short "9x13 pan equals ..." quick-answer list near the top for featured snippets. |
| 19 | Low | Logo/favicon | The icon (a rounded square with a circle and dot) is clean but does not say cake or pan. At 16 and 32 px it looks like a generic app icon. No `favicon.ico` in the root. | Draw a round pan seen from above next to a square pan, with a spoon or a cake slice line; keep the brand colour. Add a 48 px `favicon.ico`. |
| 20 | Low | Visual hierarchy | Desktop: the two forms take most of the first screen; the factor is below it at 1280x900 (only its top is visible). The block "Number of pans" sits alone in a row next to an empty cell. | Place Number of pans beside Depth, or put the result above the forms on desktop in a sticky right column. |
| 21 | Low | Bake guide | "Start checking at 30 min, expect done by 40 min" for a similar-depth pan (35 min recipe, factor 0.86) is a wide window and reads like a promise. The guide label is present, which is good. | Narrow the range for same-depth pans (for example plus or minus 5 min) and keep the "This is a guide" line right under the numbers. |
| 22 | Low | Print | Print view hides article and FAQ but the scaled list prints in mono with the input box hidden-looking. Not checked on paper. | Check one print preview; give the scaled list its own printable block with the pan names and factor at the top. |
| 23 | Low | Privacy page | "Last updated" is the build date, so it changes with every deploy. | Hard-code the real date. |
| 24 | Low | Dark mode | Good contrast overall (text on brand-soft, muted text OK; Lighthouse accessibility 100). The pink brand-ink on near-black is a little soft for the 1.27x number. | Optional: lift `--brand-ink` in dark mode by 5 to 8%. |

## Notes (checked, no action)
- Title, canonical (`https://cakepanconverter.com/`), OG and Twitter tags with a 1200x630 image, theme-color, manifest, apple-touch icon, robots.txt and sitemap-index are all present and correct. `_headers` noindexes pages.dev.
- Layout has no horizontal scroll at 390. Dark and light both work, theme is set before paint.
- cm mode math is right (8" round 323.7 cm2 vs 9" 411.9 cm2, 1.27x) apart from the drift in row 1.
- Bundt, sheet, loaf and deeper-pan paths give sensible output and temperature drops (325F for deeper batter, 15 min earlier for sheet).
